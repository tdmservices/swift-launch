HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

White-label shipping with Swift Launch is effortless from your side and flawless from your customer's side. We set up your branded packaging, configure your carrier accounts, and integrate with your stores — then handle every dispatch invisibly and professionally. Your customers get a premium branded delivery experience every time, and you get all the credit. Nobody knows we were ever involved.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Brand Setup & Consultation

We learn your brand identity, packaging preferences, and shipping requirements — configuring your white-label profile so every dispatch reflects your brand perfectly from day one.

STEP 02 : Packaging & Materials Sourcing

We source or work with your existing branded packaging, custom tissue, inserts, and labels — ensuring every element of the unboxing experience is on-brand and professional.

STEP 03 : Store Integration & Order Flow

Your platforms are connected to our dispatch system — Amazon, eBay, Shopify, and TikTok Shop orders flow in automatically and are processed under your brand instantly.

STEP 04 : Branded Pick, Pack & Dispatch

Every order is picked and packed using your branded materials and dispatched via the best carrier for that shipment — with your branding on all labels and documentation.

STEP 05 : Tracking & Customer Communication

Tracking details are sent to your customers from your brand — maintaining a seamless, professional post-purchase experience that builds loyalty and drives repeat business.
