HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

Building a high-converting store requires more than good design — it takes a structured process, e-commerce expertise, and meticulous attention to detail. Swift Launch takes your brief from concept to launch with complete efficiency. We keep you involved at every stage, deliver on time, and hand over a store that's fully operational, beautifully designed, and built to drive revenue from day one.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Discovery & Brief

We learn your brand, products, target audience, and goals — building a clear project scope, timeline, and design direction before a single line of code is written.

STEP 02 : Design & Wireframing

We create detailed wireframes and design mockups for your approval — mapping out every page layout, section, and user journey before development begins.

STEP 03 : Development & Build

Our developers build your store to the approved design — writing clean code, configuring your Shopify settings, and integrating every required app and payment gateway.

STEP 04 : Testing & Quality Check

Every page, button, form, and checkout flow is tested across all devices and browsers — ensuring a flawless experience for every customer before launch day.

STEP 05 : Launch & Handover

Your store goes live on schedule. We handle the launch, provide a full walkthrough, and offer ongoing support so you're never left figuring things out alone.
