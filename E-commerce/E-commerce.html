HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

Getting started with Swift Launch E-Commerce Management is straightforward. We begin by learning everything about your brand, your current setup, and your goals. From there, we build a tailored management strategy and take over your accounts completely — handling every platform, every listing, every campaign. You approve the plan and watch the results come in.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Discovery call

We learn your brand, goals, platforms, and current account status in a focused 45-minute session to map the right strategy.

STEP 02 : Account Audit

We conduct a full audit of your existing listings, ads, and account health to identify gaps, risks, and quick-win opportunities.

STEP 03 : Strategy & Onboarding

We build a tailored management plan, get access set up, and brief the full team assigned to your account.

STEP 04 : Full Management Goes Live

Listings optimised, ads launched, inventory tracked. Your account is now actively managed by our specialist team.

STEP 05 : Reporting & Growth Review

Monthly performance reports, strategy calls, and continuous optimisation to drive consistent growth month after month.
