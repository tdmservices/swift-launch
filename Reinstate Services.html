HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

Account suspensions feel catastrophic — but with the right team, most are completely reversible. Swift Launch follows a proven, platform-specific reinstatement process built from dozens of successful appeals across Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, and Shopify. We move fast, communicate clearly, and fight for your account until it's back live and fully operational.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Emergency Consultation

We review your suspension notice, account history, and performance metrics to understand exactly what triggered the suspension.

STEP 02 : Root Cause Analysis

We identify every violation — stated or unstated — and build a complete picture of what the platform is looking for in your appeal.

STEP 03 : Plan of Action Writing

We craft a detailed, policy-compliant Plan of Action that directly addresses the suspension reasons with clear corrective actions and evidence.

STEP 04 : Appeal Submission

We submit your appeal through the correct channel, in the right format, with supporting documentation to give it the best chance of success.

STEP 05 : Follow-Up & Reinstatement

We monitor the platform's response, handle any follow-up requests, and keep you updated at every stage until your account is fully restored.
