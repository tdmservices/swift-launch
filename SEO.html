HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

SEO done right is methodical, strategic, and built for the long term. Swift Launch starts with a complete audit of your current search visibility, identifies the highest-impact opportunities, and implements a structured optimisation plan across every platform you sell on. Results build month on month — turning your store into an organic traffic machine that keeps delivering.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : SEO Audit & Discovery

We conduct a full audit of your existing listings, website, and search rankings to identify exactly where you're losing visibility and revenue.

STEP 02 : Keyword Research & Mapping

We research high-volume, high-intent keywords across every platform and map them strategically to your products, pages, and content.

STEP 03 : On-Page Optimisation

Titles, bullet points, descriptions, backend fields, meta tags — every element is rewritten and optimised to rank higher and convert better.

STEP 04 : Implementation & Indexing

All optimisations are implemented correctly and submitted for indexing — ensuring search engines and platform algorithms register every change we make.

STEP 05 : Monthly Reporting & Refinement

We track ranking movements, organic traffic, and conversion impact monthly — refining the strategy continuously to drive compounding growth over time.
