HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

Wasted ad spend is one of the biggest profit killers in e-commerce. Swift Launch builds performance marketing campaigns from the ground up — starting with deep audience research and ending with continuous, data-driven optimisation. Every campaign is structured to deliver a clear, measurable return, and every pound is accounted for. We scale what wins and cut what doesn't. Every single week.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Strategy & Discovery Call

We learn your products, margins, target audience, and current ad performance to build a campaign strategy designed around real profitability.

STEP 02 : Audience & Keyword Research

We identify your highest-intent audiences and best-performing keywords across every platform — building the targeting foundation your campaigns need to win.

STEP 03 : Campaign Build & Launch

We structure, write, and launch your campaigns across the agreed platforms — with correct bidding strategies, ad formats, and budget allocation from day one.

STEP 04 : Testing & Optimisation

We continuously test creatives, audiences, bids, and placements — cutting underperformers fast and scaling winning combinations to maximise your return on every pound spent.

STEP 05 : Weekly Reporting & Review

Detailed weekly performance reports covering spend, ROAS, conversions, and revenue — with strategy adjustments made proactively to keep results moving in the right direction.
