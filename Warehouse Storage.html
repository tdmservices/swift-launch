HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

Getting your stock into our warehouse is straightforward, fast, and fully managed from the moment your inventory arrives. Swift Launch handles everything from receiving and stock counting to organising your products for rapid dispatch. You get complete visibility through your client dashboard, and your stock is always ready to fulfil orders the moment they come in — with zero delays and zero stress.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Storage Consultation

We assess your product range, dimensions, stock volume, and seasonal requirements — building a tailored storage plan that fits your inventory and your budget precisely.

STEP 02 : Onboarding & Setup

Your storage account is configured in our warehouse management system — product SKUs created, storage locations assigned, and your client dashboard set up and ready to use.

STEP 03 : Stock Delivery & Receiving

Your inventory is delivered to our Manchester warehouse — received, counted, quality-checked, and logged into your live dashboard within 24 hours of arrival on site.

STEP 04 : Organised Storage & Management

Every product is labelled, located, and organised for fast retrieval — maintained and managed by our warehouse team so your stock is always in perfect order and ready to go.

STEP 05 : Seamless Dispatch Integration

When orders come in across your platforms, your stored stock moves directly into our pick and pack process — dispatched same day with no delays or manual handoffs required.
