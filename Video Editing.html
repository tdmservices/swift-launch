HOW ITS WORK

How Its Work

Great video content doesn't happen by accident — it's built on clear briefs, skilled editing, and a deep understanding of what makes viewers stop, watch, and buy. Swift Launch takes your raw footage or content brief and transforms it into platform-ready videos that perform. Fast turnaround, unlimited revisions within scope, and a consistent quality standard that makes your brand look world-class everywhere it appears.

Schedule a Call

STEP 01 : Brief & Content Review

We gather your raw footage, brand guidelines, and platform requirements — building a clear creative brief that aligns the edit to your goals and audience.

STEP 02 : Edit & Production

Our editors cut, colour grade, and structure your video for maximum impact — adding captions, music, transitions, and motion graphics to the agreed brief.

STEP 03 : First Cut Delivery

Your first edited version is delivered within 48 hours — ready for your review, with a clear feedback process so revisions are quick and straightforward.

STEP 04 : Revisions & Refinement

We incorporate your feedback and refine the edit until it's exactly right — ensuring every video meets your brand standard before it goes anywhere near a platform.

STEP 05 : Final Delivery & Formatting

Final videos are exported in every required format and resolution — platform-ready for TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, Facebook, or wherever your content needs to go live.
