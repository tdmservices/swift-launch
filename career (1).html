HIRING PROCESS

Simple, fair, and fast.

No 8-round interviews. We respect your time and move with real intention.

📝1

Apply

Submit your application. No cover letter required — just your work and a few honest answers.

📞2

Intro Call

A 30-minute casual conversation with the hiring lead. No prep needed — we just want to get to know you.

🎯3

Skills Review

A focused, paid take-home task that reflects real work you'd actually do in this role. No trick questions.

👥4

Team Interview

Meet 2–3 of your future teammates. We talk culture, collaboration, and how you work — not just what you know.

🎉5

Offer

Decision within 48 hours. Straightforward offer, no games, no ghosting — ever.
